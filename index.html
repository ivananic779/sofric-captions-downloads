The first time you open it

Windows will show a blue box saying the publisher is unknown. That is expected — the app is not code-signed yet.

Click More info, then Run anyway. It only asks once.

Needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.

The first time you open it

Open the downloaded file and drag Sofric Captions into your Applications folder, then open it from there.

macOS will refuse the first time. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and press Open Anyway.

If it still will not open, open Terminal and paste:
xattr -cr "/Applications/Sofric Captions.app"

It only asks once. Needs macOS 13 Ventura or newer; the download works on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Before you start

The first time you transcribe, the app downloads its speech model (640 MB), once. After that it works offline, and your videos never leave the computer.

Your edit is never changed: the captions are saved beside it, and every export is a new file.
